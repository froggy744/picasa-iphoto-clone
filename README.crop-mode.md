# PIC Guide 5 — Crop Mode: Trim & Straighten

*Part of the [PIC release candidate guides](README.release.candidate.md).*

Crop Mode is PIC's little trimming workshop — a drag-anywhere crop box, a rule-of-thirds grid and a straighten slider. Nothing more, nothing less. Like everything in PIC's editor, cropping is **non-destructive**: your original photo is never cut, and you can reset at any time.

---

## Opening Crop Mode

1. Select a photo and open **Edit Mode** (the ✏️ pencil in the info bar, or right-click → Edit).
2. Click the **Crop…** button under *Geometry* — or switch to the **Crop** tab in the left panel.

Your photo appears with a bright **crop rectangle** over it, ready to shape.

<img src="samples/Crop.jpg" alt="Crop Mode with the crop rectangle" width="900">

## Drag Your Crop

- **Drag the corners and edges** of the rectangle to resize it.
- **Drag inside** the rectangle to move it around the photo.
- The **rule-of-thirds grid** helps you place your subject — put eyes and horizons on the lines for a naturally balanced composition.

Everything outside the rectangle dims slightly, so you always see exactly what will be kept.

## Pick a Ready-Made Shape

<img src="samples/Theme-Aqua.jpg" alt="Aspect ratio choices" width="900">

Don't want to drag? Choose an **aspect ratio** and the crop box snaps to it:

| Shape | Great for |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | Crop to any shape you like |
| **Original** | Keep the photo's own proportions |
| **1:1** | Square — profile pictures, social media |
| **4:3** | Classic photo prints and slideshows |
| **3:2** | The classic 35 mm film shape |
| **16:9** | Widescreen — TVs and monitors |
| **A4 / US Letter** | Printing on standard paper |

Alongside the ratios you can also flip the **Orientation** between **Landscape** and **Portrait** with one click.

## Straighten — Level It Perfectly

Is the horizon doing its own thing? The **Straighten slider** rotates the photo in tiny amounts while you watch. Combined with the grid overlay, getting a perfectly level shot takes five seconds.

## Apply It — or Change Your Mind

When the crop box looks right:

- Click **Apply Crop** — the photo is cropped in your library view. Remember: the *original file* on disk is never touched; the crop is saved as part of PIC's non-destructive edit recipe.
- **Reset Crop** brings back the full photo.
- **Cancel** leaves Crop Mode without changing anything.

Changed your mind a week later? Open Edit Mode and hit **Reset** — the full original comes right back, because it was never actually cut.
