I loved Google Picasa because it was one of the fastest photo apps I ever used and the minimal design of iLife/iPhoto always looked good to me. I got tired of using emulated apps to run Picasa and so as more of us move to Linux, and with AI changing what is possible I build an app that will suit my needs. I remembered a YouTuber saying: “If you don’t have the app, just write it.” So I did — with help from Codex, GPT, Qwen and anything else I could throw at it. I’m not a coder, just a nerd who loves sport, photography, and has wanted to write code since the days of the movie Hackers.
